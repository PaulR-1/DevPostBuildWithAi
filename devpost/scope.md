# FocusRot

A review session that can tell you have left the work, from where your eyes go and how you are sitting.

## The Unique Kernel

FocusRot watches your gaze and posture and can tell when you are not on the work. You show it where the computer or the notebook is, you see yourself, and it notices when you drift. The loud snap-back, the meme on your own camera feed, and the shareable snapshot of your surprised face are the rest of the idea. Those wait. Without gaze and posture, none of that has anything to react to.

## Who It's For

You, while reviewing. You look away, check your phone, or leave the work, and the time disappears. Today, nothing notices.

## The Core Loop

Open the app. See yourself on the camera. Calibrate it so it knows where the computer or the materials are. Start the way you start a run. It watches where your eyes are and how you are sitting. You can tell whether it thinks you are on the work.

## Inspiration & Identity

Starting a session should feel like starting a run in Strava (https://www.strava.com): pick the session, then press start. The larger mood is playful and a little unhinged: a meme or a sound on the camera feed, and friends sharing a snapshot of your surprised face when you look back. That mood is part of the idea. It is not part of this proof.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

"I'd use it to review."

## What "Working" Looks Like

You are on camera. After calibration, the app shows your gaze and posture clearly enough that a one-minute demo can prove it knows when you are on the work and when you have left it. That visible catch is the moment.

## The POC Boundary

In this proof: the camera view of yourself, calibration for where the computer or the notebook is, gaze, posture, a session you start, and a clear on-screen sign of whether you are on the work.

If it is not needed to prove that catch, it is not in this proof.

## Later

- A chance to come back, then an intervention at an intensity you set: a meme or a sound on the feed of yourself.
- Fun public shaming: a friend can take a snapshot of your surprised face as you look back at the screen and share it. An MP4 option stays a note for later.
- Shared rooms, inviting friends, and collaboration.
- A phone border and a floating circle while you are in another app. An Android phone can draw that overlay after you allow it. An iPhone cannot.
- Tab and page tracking, and attention span as its own measure.
- Points and unlockable collectibles. A day streak is on the home page now. It only counts days that already have a saved session.

## Explicitly Cut

- Treating every look away as an instant failure. You want a chance to come back before any intervention. The intervention itself is later. The instant-fail version from the original PDF is out.
- An iPhone app that draws a border and a floating circle over other apps. An iPhone cannot do that, so this project does not promise it.
